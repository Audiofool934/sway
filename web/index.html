Play music with your hands

You decide when and how. Sway keeps every note in time and in key, and a band follows your lead.

Right hand · your voice

  • Height picks the note on the ladder
  • Pinch plays it; move while pinched to draw a melody

Left hand · the band

  • Height sets the energy, from air to peak
  • Fist cuts the band; open it to bring them back
  • Pinch and hold loops the last four bars you played

Hold two fists together to end the piece.
