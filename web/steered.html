YOU LEAD · AN ORCHESTRA PLAYS

Score the scene with your hands.

An orchestra, generated on this Mac, plays a film score that grows as you play. Your hands lead it, and an arranger plans what comes next.

  1. 01
    Raise or lower your hands

    Low hands bring cellos and basses; high hands bring violins.

  2. 02
    Move more, or less

    Quick, wide movement makes it lively. Slow movement keeps it calm.

  3. 03
    Spread your hands apart

    Close hands keep the melody on the chord. Wide hands open it to the whole scale.

  4. 04
    Hold still

    The music thins to a quiet chord. Move again to grow it back.

  5. 05
    Clap

    The music moves on to its next section, on the next bar.

The music is generated on this Mac, and the camera image stays here. To plan what comes next, the arranger may describe the music to Qwen, in the cloud: in words and numbers, never an image.
