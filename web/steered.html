YOU CONDUCT · AN ORCHESTRA PLAYS

Conduct the orchestra with your hands.

An orchestra, generated on this Mac, plays a film score that a composer writes as you go. You decide how it is played.

  1. 01
    Conduct with your right hand

    Bigger motion plays louder, and faster motion drives harder.

  2. 02
    Bring a voice forward with your left

    Point toward the violins, the brass, or the cellos and basses, and raise your hand to bring them forward.

  3. 03
    Open your palm, or pinch

    An open palm plays long lines; a pinch plays short notes.

  4. 04
    Make a fist, or hold still

    The music hushes to a quiet chord.

  5. 05
    Raise both hands, then bring them down

    The orchestra moves on to its next section, on the beat.

The music is generated on this Mac, and the camera image stays here. To write what comes next, the composer may describe the music to Qwen, in the cloud: in words and numbers, never an image.
